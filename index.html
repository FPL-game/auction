FAQ — How This League Works
The Basics
How does the draft work?+

Everyone bids live on players, one at a time, until all 6 squads are full. It's a real auction — whoever bids highest gets the player, no snake-draft turns.

What's the budget?+

Everyone starts with 100m to build an 11-player squad. Spend it however you like — there's no rule on how much to spend per position.

Do I need a specific formation?+

No. The only rule is 1 goalkeeper. The other 10 spots can be any mix of defenders, midfielders and forwards you want.

Can two teams own the same player?+

No. Once a player is bought, they're off the market for everyone else all season.

Is there a bench?+

No bench. All 11 players you own are your starting XI every Gameweek. If one of them doesn't play, you just take the zero.

Scoring & The Table
How are points calculated?+

Standard FPL points — whatever each player scores in real Fantasy Premier League that Gameweek is what counts for your team.

Do captains or chips apply?+

No captaincy and no chips (no Wildcard, Bench Boost, Triple Captain, Free Hit). Just your 11 players' raw points added up.

How do fixtures work?+

One match per Gameweek slot, just like real Premier League weekends — 3 games happen simultaneously each GW, and every team plays every other team once every 5 Gameweeks, repeating all season.

How do I win a match?+

Whoever's 11 players score more total points that Gameweek wins. 3 points for a win, 1 each for a draw, 0 for a loss.

How are ties on the table broken?+

First by total points scored across the season, then by points differential (points for minus points against).

Transfers & Waivers
Can I change my squad during the season?+

Yes. You can release a player any time (no fee) and replace them through a mini-bidding process.

How does replacing a player work?+

You announce who you're releasing. That opens a 24-hour window where anyone — including you — can bid to bring in the replacement.

What do I bid with?+

A separate waiver budget: 20m per team, plus whatever you didn't spend from your original 100m draft budget.

When can transfers happen?+

Any time, except during a locked window — from 6 hours before the first game of a Gameweek until that Gameweek finishes.

Can a transfer be blocked?+

Yes. If 2 or more of the 6 managers vote that a transfer is unfair, it's overturned.

Admin
Who runs the league?+

Claude keeps the live leaderboard on this site and syncs real FPL scores automatically every 2 hours during Gameweeks, so anything can be corrected quickly if there's a mistake.

Where do I check live scores?+

The Live Scores tab — it reflects the current Gameweek's matches based on real FPL data as it comes in.
